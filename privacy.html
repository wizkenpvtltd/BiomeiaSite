Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 24 August 2026. This page has not yet had legal review; see the note at the end.

This page explains what happens when you leave your name and email with Biomeia on this pre-launch site. We collect the minimum needed to tell you when Biomeia launches in Singapore, and nothing more.

What we collect

  • The name and email address you enter in the sign-up form.
  • The market you registered interest for (currently: Singapore).
  • The date and time of your submission.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising on this pre-launch site, and we do not collect payment information here — there is nothing to buy yet.

How we use it

  • To notify you when the first Singapore batch is ready.
  • To send the founder's note ahead of the public launch.
  • To understand how much interest exists in each market, in aggregate.

We will not sell your information. We will not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time. Every launch email will include an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us directly.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hello@biomeia.com.

Open item: this policy is a working draft written for the pre-launch signup page only. Before the product ships, it needs review by a Singapore-qualified privacy counsel — in particular to confirm the data-processing basis, any cross-border transfer to Japan or other markets, and retention periods, and to extend it to cover checkout, shipping and customer-service data once the store goes live.
