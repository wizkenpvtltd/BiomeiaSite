The Problem

You've tried everything. None of it was aimed at the skin.

  • Heavy oils

    Sit on the scalp and clog it, then need stripping back off again.

  • Clarifying shampoos

    Strong enough to leave the skin tight, raw and reactive.

  • Hair serums

    Built for length and strands, delivered nowhere near the roots.

  • Specialist visits

    Clinics, dermatologists — and still no clear answer to take home.

Every one of them treats the hair. The skin it grows from goes unaddressed.

The Skin-First Logic

You double-cleanse your face. Why are you skipping your scalp?

Your scalp is skin — an extension of your facial skin, with a higher density of oil glands, sitting beneath a canopy of hair. That canopy quietly traps oil, sweat and buildup against the skin underneath.

Biomeia does for the scalp what a micellar cleanser does for the face — dissolve buildup beneath the surface, then rinse away completely. No heavy residue, no stripping.

  • Heavy oils nourish the roots
    They suffocate the follicle, trapping buildup beneath the hair.
  • A stronger shampoo strips it off
    Harsh surfactants damage the barrier and trigger reactive oiliness.

The Pre-Wash Ritual

Five steps, once or twice a week

Before you shampoo — not instead of it.

  1. Prepare

    Part dry, unwashed hair into sections.

  2. Apply

    Twist the applicator to ON, then glide the comb tip along the scalp — the multi-port head dispenses oil onto skin, not onto hair.

  3. Activate

    Massage gently with fingertips for 1–3 minutes to dissolve buildup.

  4. Rest

    Leave on for 10–15 minutes.

  5. Rinse

    Add water to emulsify into a milky rinse, then follow with a mild shampoo. Twist back to OFF before storing.
