Our Story

Hair care starts with scalp soil.

Long before founding Biomeia, our founder experienced early hair thinning and an erratic scalp. She moved between doctors for years without a clear answer — the breakthrough came instead from a hairdresser, who pointed past the hair shaft to the scalp itself. That pivot, from treating hair to treating the skin beneath it, became the founding insight for the brand.

"For years, I thought I was doing something wrong. I tried heavy traditional oils that clogged my scalp, and harsh shampoos that left it raw and irritated." — Founder, Biomeia

"When you're young and dealing with hair concerns, it's easy to feel like you're doing something wrong, or that you're the only one going through it," she says. "I spent years running from pillar to post — expensive scalp clinics, dermatologists, wondering whether it was related to my environment, water quality, or internal health — hoping someone would finally have the answer. And the whole time, nobody was saying the one thing that would have changed everything for me: your scalp is skin. It deserves the same gentleness, the same barrier care, that you'd give your face without a second thought."

Realizing that heavy traditional oils and harsh clarifying shampoos were both working against the scalp's natural biology, she partnered with Japanese formulation labs to build a pre-wash treatment based on the same double-cleansing logic used in facial skincare.

The bridge

"People naturally raise an eyebrow when you talk about skin-first logic for the scalp," she explains. "They think, 'My face doesn't grow a lot of hair, so how can the rules be the same?' But biologically, your scalp is an extension of your facial skin — it actually has a higher density of oil glands. The difference is that your hair acts like a canopy, quietly trapping oil, sweat, and buildup against the skin underneath. Dousing your head in heavy traditional oil is a bit like slathering thick butter on your face and hoping your pores just… behave. They won't. And they never did, for me."

Engineered in Japan

To solve this without over-promising miracle fixes, Biomeia worked directly with cosmetic formulation labs in Japan, leaning into Kodawari — an uncompromising diligence to craftsmanship and purpose. The result: a waterless pre-wash scalp cleansing oil, built on facial-grade olive-derived esters and a 5-ceramide barrier complex, delivered through an integrated applicator designed to bypass hair length entirely.

"I didn't want to create another over-hyped beauty brand promising magic overnight transformations. I just wanted to build the thing I needed and couldn't find." — Founder, Biomeia

Hair care starts with scalp soil. We didn't create Biomeia to sell overnight miracles or promise quick fixes. We built it to validate a shared struggle and offer honest, thoughtful scalp care that respects your skin barrier — so you finally feel heard, understood, and cared for.
