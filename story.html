A Founder's Statement

Hair care starts with scalp soil.

“I didn't want to build another over-hyped beauty brand promising magic overnight. I wanted the thing I needed and couldn't find.”

Romanshi, Founder

For years, I thought I was doing something wrong.

My hair started thinning early, and my scalp never behaved — oily one week, tight and irritated the next, with no pattern I could find. So I did what you do. I went to doctors, then more doctors. Expensive scalp clinics. Dermatologists. I wondered whether it was the water, the weather, something happening inside me. Every time, I hoped this would be the appointment with the answer in it.

It wasn't a doctor who finally said the useful thing. It was my hairdresser — who looked past the hair entirely and told me to pay attention to the scalp underneath it.

Your scalp is skin. Nobody had said it to me. It changed everything.

Which sounds almost too obvious. But sit with what it means. You wouldn't put anything on your face without asking whether it respects your skin barrier. Meanwhile I'd spent years pouring heavy traditional oils onto my scalp, where they sat and clogged, then scrubbing them back off with shampoos harsh enough to leave the skin raw. Two opposite treatments, both working against the same biology.

The part people push back on

When I talk about skin-first logic for the scalp, most people raise an eyebrow. The response is usually some version of: my face doesn't grow a lot of hair, so how can the rules be the same?

Biologically, though, your scalp is an extension of your facial skin. It has a higher density of oil glands, not fewer. What's different is the canopy — your hair sits over all of it, quietly trapping oil, sweat and buildup against the skin.

Putting heavy oil on top of that is a bit like slathering thick butter on your face and hoping your pores behave. They won't. Mine never did.

Why Japan

I didn't want to guess my way to a formula, so I went to cosmetic formulation labs in Japan and worked with them directly. What I found there was Kodawari — an uncompromising diligence to craftsmanship and purpose — and it shaped how the whole thing got made.

What came out of it is a waterless pre-wash cleansing oil: facial-grade olive-derived esters, a 5-ceramide barrier complex, and an applicator built into the tube so the formula reaches your scalp rather than your hair length. It takes the double-cleansing logic you already use on your face and puts it where nobody was using it.

Hair care starts with scalp soil. I'm not going to promise you an overnight transformation — I spent too many years being promised those. What I can offer is honest, careful scalp care that treats your skin as skin. And if you've been running from pillar to post the way I was, quietly wondering whether it's just you: it isn't. That's most of why this exists.

RomanshiFounder, Biomeia
